🎥 Connect OBS Studio to BobaStream

You can pipe your live OBS broadcast into BobaStream using either of these quick methods:

Option 1: Stream to Twitch / YouTube (Zero Lag & Free)

1. Stream from OBS to your Twitch or YouTube account.
2. Select Twitch/YouTube on the feed dropdown and type your channel name.

Option 2: OBS Virtual Camera

1. In OBS, click Start Virtual Camera.
2. On BobaStream, switch feed to OBS Virtual Cam / Webcam.
